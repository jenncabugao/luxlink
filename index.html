Community-powered coordination for shelter animals

Help shelter communities act faster when animals need them.

LuxLink is a mobile-first coordination platform for municipal shelters and the communities that support them. It helps coordinate foster placements, temporary takeovers, transport, adoption-hours support, and volunteer needs in one place.

Learn more Our mission

Built around the people who show up.

Municipal shelters often have dedicated communities of vetted fosters, transporters, volunteers, donors, and staff. LuxLink gives those communities a clearer way to coordinate needs, respond quickly, and know when help is still needed.

Targeted mobile alerts

Notify the right people when a foster, takeover, transport, or volunteer need comes up.

Live coordination

See who offered, who was contacted, and when a need is actually covered.

Community recognition

Celebrate the people who step up and reinforce the community that makes shelter support possible.

Mission

At its core, LuxLink is built to help municipal shelters mobilize the caring communities that make better outcomes for animals possible.

Starting with foster coordination.

LuxLink is beginning with the workflows municipal shelters already rely on every day: foster placement, short-term takeovers, transport, adoption-hours coverage, and community volunteer asks.

LuxLink is currently in development.
